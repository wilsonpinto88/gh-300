🏛️Organisation vs enterprise control

Organisation level

  • Policies for one org and its members
VS

Enterprise level

  • Policies across all orgs
  • Override org policy unless delegated
❗
Enterprise policies override organisation policies unless delegation is allowed.

🚦Model and feature restrictions

  • ✅Limit access to advanced or premium models
  • ✅Enable or disable features such as chat or coding agents
  • ✅Restricting models controls premium usage and cost
  • ✅Same constraints for all users
